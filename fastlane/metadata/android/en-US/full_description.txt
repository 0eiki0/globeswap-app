Take a photo of whatever you see right now – GlobeSwap sends it to a stranger somewhere in the world who just took a photo too, and you get theirs.

📸 SHOOT & SWAP
Take a photo, tap Swap, and receive a surprise photo from someone else on the planet. A sunset in Lisbon, a busy street in Tokyo, a cat in Buenos Aires – you never know what comes next.

💬 CONVERSATIONS MADE OF PICTURES
Reply with a photo and keep the conversation going – without a single word, if you like. All your swaps are waiting for you in your chats.

🎨 MAKE IT YOURS
Add text, emoji stickers and drawings, pick a filter, and resize and rotate everything with your fingers before you send.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No sign-up: no name, no email, no phone number
• Location and other hidden photo data are removed from every photo
• Photos can’t be saved, and screenshots are blocked
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Delete all your data in the app at any time

🛡️ SAFE COMMUNITY
Clear rules, reporting and blocking built in, and active moderation. GlobeSwap is for people aged 16 and older.

Start swapping and see the world through other people’s eyes!
