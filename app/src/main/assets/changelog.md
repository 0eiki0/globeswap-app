- GlobeSwap now speaks German – the app follows your phone's language (German or English)

- Achievements on your world map, and how much of the world you've seen compared to others

## 2.7 (2026-10-25)
- Give yourself a name if you like (Settings → Your name) – people you chat with see it instead of "Stranger"
- Backup code: get your account and chats back after reinstalling (Settings → Backup code)
- See what changed in every version on this page

## 2.6 (2026-10-25)
- Camera: pinch to zoom, zoom buttons, ultra-wide and tap to focus

## 2.5 (2026-10-24)
- The "New photo" notification now disappears once you've read your chats

## 2.4 (2026-10-24)
- Simpler age check – you accept the privacy policy on the next screen

## 2.3 (2026-10-24)
- Installed from GitHub? GlobeSwap now updates itself

## 2.2 (2026-10-24)
- The age check now asks for your full date of birth

## 2.1 (2026-10-24)
- See the country of every photo, and your own world map of countries you got photos from – you can switch your country off in Settings
- Swap with anyone, people nearby or people far away
- A daily theme above the camera for inspiration
- React to photos: double-tap for ❤️, long-press for more
- End a chat without blocking the other person
- Age check before the first start
- Chats now always open at the newest photo

## 2.0 (2026-10-24)
- Instant notifications for new photos

## 1.9 (2026-10-24)
- Deleted photos leave a short note in the chat

## 1.8 (2026-10-23)
- Only the latest 10 photos of a chat are kept

## 1.7 (2026-10-23)
- Chats take turns: you can reply once the stranger has sent a photo

## 1.6 (2026-10-23)
- Delete whole chats and your own photos – for the other person too

## 1.5 (2026-10-23)
- More privacy: photos can't be saved, screenshots and screen recordings are blocked
- A notice when a new version is available

## 1.4 (2026-10-23)
- Smaller download, and GlobeSwap is now free software (GPL-3.0)

## 1.3 (2026-10-23)
- First release: swap photos with strangers around the world
